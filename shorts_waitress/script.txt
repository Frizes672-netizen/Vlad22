A boy wasn't allowed to eat at a restaurant — and that's the only reason he's still alive.

Florida, New Year's Day. A family sits down at a table. The adults get their food. The eleven-year-old boy gets nothing.

The waitress asks: "What about him?" "He'll eat at home."

Then she notices the bruises. On his arms. On his face.

She can't just ask — his parents are right there. So she does something most people would never think of.

She writes on a notepad: "Do you need help?" — and stands behind his parents, where only he can see it.

The boy reads the note. And gives the smallest nod.

She calls 911.

It turned out his stepfather had been beating him — and starving him as punishment.

The stepfather was arrested, and later found guilty in court.

The police chief said: without her, they'd probably be investigating a homicide.

Her name is Flaviane Carvalho. Florida named a day in her honor.

One note. One nod.

Sometimes, saving a life takes nothing more than asking in a way only they can answer.
