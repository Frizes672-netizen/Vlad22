Your car just went into a lake. And the first thing most people do is exactly what kills them.

The water is at your doors. You have about sixty seconds.

Do you call 911 — or open the door?

You have three seconds.

Neither.

The water pressure holds the door shut. You won't open it. And the call burns the only minute you have.

Seatbelt off. Side window down — now, while the electrics still work. If it won't go down, break it near the corner. Not the windshield.

Kids out first, oldest first. Then you. Then swim up.

Seatbelt. Window. Children. Out.

You made it out. But it's November, the water was ice cold, and the nearest house is a mile away.
