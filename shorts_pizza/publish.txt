TITLE: She called 911 to order a pizza — and it saved her mom 🍕 #shorts
DESCRIPTION: A true story. Oregon, Ohio, November 2019. A caller "ordered a pizza" from 911 — dispatcher Tim Teneyck realized she couldn't talk and sent police with their sirens off. "Pizza" isn't an official code. He just listened.
If you can't talk, in many places you can text 911 — check if it's available where you live.
Sources: NBC News, CNN, TODAY, WTOP (2019).
