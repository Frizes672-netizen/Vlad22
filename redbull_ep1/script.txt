He Took on Red Bull Over Its "Gives You Wings" Slogan. Here's What Really Happened.
Imagine buying an energy drink because you believe the advertising.
You crack open a can, take a sip, and wait for that extra boost of energy.
But nothing crazy happens.
You don't suddenly become a superhero. You don't grow wings. And you definitely don't start flying around your neighborhood.
Now, imagine taking one of the world's biggest energy drink companies to court over the claims behind its advertising.
Sounds ridiculous, right?
Well, something surprisingly close to that actually happened.
In 2014, Red Bull agreed to a settlement worth up to 13 million dollars to resolve a class-action lawsuit over its advertising.
And no, this wasn't a case of one guy walking into court demanding a pair of wings.
The real story involved claims about energy, performance, consumer protection, and a legal fight that attracted attention across the country.
But here's the part most people get wrong.
One man did not personally walk away with 13 million dollars.
