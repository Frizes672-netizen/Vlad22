The maid: "I finished this floor at eight. Then I was in the laundry room all night."

The man next door: "I went to bed at nine. I slept right through it."

The night guard: "I watched the hallway camera. Nobody came in or out."

You have three seconds.

It's the man next door.

Look at his robe pocket. That little gold thing isn't a button. It's the watch's bracelet.

And his bed? Still perfectly made. He never went to bed.

He went through the connecting door — so the hallway camera never saw him.
