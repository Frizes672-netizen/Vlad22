Someone stole a two-million-dollar gold watch from room 1208. One of these three is lying. Can you spot who?

The maid: "I finished this floor at eight. Then I was in the laundry room all night."

The man next door: "I went to bed at nine. I slept right through it."

The night guard: "I watched the hallway camera. Nobody came in or out."

But wait. The maid has a key to every room. And one of the guard's cameras went dark at 11:40.

So who is it? You have five seconds.

It's the man next door.

That gold "button" on his robe pocket is the watch's bracelet.

His bed was never slept in — and the connecting door leads right into 1208.

Watch again.
