Someone stole a two-million-dollar watch from room 1208. One of these three is lying. Can you spot who?

The maid: "I finished this floor at eight. Then I was in the laundry room all night."

The man next door: "I went to bed at nine. I slept right through it."

The night guard: "I watched the hallway camera. Nobody came in or out."

It's the man next door.

He says he slept all night — but look at his bed. Still perfectly made. And the chocolate the maid left on his pillow at eight… is still there.

He never went to bed. He went through the connecting door.
