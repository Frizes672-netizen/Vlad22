You can rewind time ten seconds. But only ten times. Ever.

Morning. Coffee on your white shirt. Rewind. Nine left.

Elevator doors shut in your face. Rewind. Eight.

A dumb joke in front of your boss. Rewind. Seven.

At night you text your ex: "Miss you". Rewind. Six.

You spend them like pocket change. Five. Four. Three. Two.

But that evening, it mattered.

A crosswalk. A boy chases his ball — straight into a truck's path.

You're too late. Brakes scream.

You have one rewind left.

You rewind. Ten seconds. The ball rolls out again.

You run. You grab him by the hood. The truck misses by a foot.

His mom can't say a word.

And your wrist turns to zero.

Nine times you saved a shirt, an elevator, your pride. Once — a life.

What would you spend your last one on?
