What happens if you don't eat… for 382 days?

In 1965, a 456-pound man walked into a hospital in Scotland. His name was Angus Barbieri. He asked the doctors: "Let me stop eating." They agreed. For a few days.

He never stopped.

Day one. Hunger. Headaches.

Day three. The hunger fades. His body starts burning its own fat.

Week two. He goes back to work at his family's fish and chip shop. Frying food he can't eat.

He lives on water, tea, coffee, and vitamins.

And the strangest part? He goes to the bathroom once every 40 to 50 days.

Day 382. His first meal: a boiled egg and bread with butter.

He lost 276 pounds. And kept it off.

Never try this without doctors.

Could you go one day?
