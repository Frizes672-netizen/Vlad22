To survive a night in the jungle, you'll have to sleep in a tree.

You've lost your group. Your radio is at the bottom of a river. Four hours until dark.

The ground is out. Snakes, ants, and a damp that brings fever by morning.

You have a machete and a coil of rope.

Find four trunks close together. Cut poles as thick as your arm. Lash them above your head — with a knot that tightens under weight.

Bamboo on top. A roof of palm leaves, overlapping like tiles, steep so the rain runs off.

A smoky fire underneath keeps the mosquitoes away.

At midnight you hear rushing water.

In the morning you look down. Where you almost slept — a river, waist-deep.
