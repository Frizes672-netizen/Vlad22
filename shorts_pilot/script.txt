At 17,000 feet, the windshield blew out… and the pilot was sucked out of the plane.

June 1990. British Airways Flight 5390. 81 passengers on board.

Bang. The captain's window explodes. Captain Tim Lancaster is ripped out of his seat. Half his body is outside the plane — in freezing wind, at hundreds of miles an hour.

A flight attendant grabs his legs. And doesn't let go.

The co-pilot takes control and dives for the nearest airport.

For twenty minutes, the crew holds on. They're sure he's dead. But they refuse to let him go.

The plane lands in Southampton.

And Tim Lancaster… is alive. Frostbite. Broken bones.

Five months later, he was back in the cockpit.
