Someone stole a three-hundred-thousand-dollar painting — and the alarm never went off. One of these three is lying. Can you spot who?

The night guard: "The power died at ten. The cameras died. I never left my desk."

The cleaner: "I was mopping the second floor. I didn't hear a thing."

The art student: "I left at six. Way before the storm even started."

But wait. The guard has a key to every door. And the cleaner's bucket is big enough to hide a rolled-up canvas.

So who is it? You have five seconds.

It's the art student.

Look at her umbrella. It's soaking wet.

The storm only hit at nine-forty. She never left.
